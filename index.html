What's a
Conversational Pace?

A conversational pace, as the name suggests, should be slow enough that you can speak in full sentences and hold a conversation with someone. However, it may be hard to determine what that feels like. This is why you have to listen to your breath. If you can feel and hear yourself breathing heavily, the chances are you may be going a little fast.

Is Conversational Pace and the Same Thing?

The short answer to this question is yes – while working in zone 2, you will usually also be able to hold a conversation and feel comfortable. However, it's important to remember that many factors from temperature, terrain and elevation, through to your current fitness level, stress level, and even how much caffeine you've had, can all raise your heart rate, even when you're running at a pace that feels conversational.
